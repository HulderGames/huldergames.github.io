INTRODUCING

SolBound

Bound inside a three-body system, fight to survive and escape.

SolBound is a turn-based 4X strategy game where energy is everything. Build a power grid, raise an army and race your rivals off a planet where day and night never stop moving.

STORY CAMPAIGN·SKIRMISH VS AI·UP TO 8 PLAYERS·COMING Q1 2028

A WORLD THAT TURNS

Daylight sweeps across the map as the planet rotates. Solar power comes and goes, so plan for the night and strike while rivals run low.

BUILD YOUR POWER GRID

Lay cables tile by tile to connect generators, batteries and barracks. Tap into enemy batteries, or cut their lines to leave them in the dark.

TWO WAYS TO WIN

Rebuild and launch your ship before anyone else, or hunt down the enemy engineers. Lose yours, and the game is over.
